HOMEMADE ICE CREAM

A creamy, sweet dessert to make your day special!

About This Recipe

Homemade ice cream is a delicious frozen dessert with a smooth and creamy texture. This simple no-churn recipe uses only a few ingredients and does not require an ice cream machine. Enjoy it on a warm day or as a sweet treat after a meal.

Preparation

15 minutes

Freezing

6 hours

Servings

4 people

Ingredients

Gather these ingredients before you begin:

  • 2 cups cold heavy whipping cream
  • 1 can (about 395 g) sweetened condensed milk
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • Chocolate chips or fresh fruit (optional)

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Prepare the bowl: Use a clean, chilled mixing bowl to help the cream whip properly.
  2. Whip the cream: Beat the cold whipping cream until it becomes thick and forms soft peaks.
  3. Mix the condensed milk: In another bowl, combine the condensed milk and vanilla extract.
  4. Combine the mixtures: Gently fold the whipped cream into the condensed milk mixture until smooth.
  5. Add your favorite flavors: Stir in chocolate chips or small pieces of fruit if desired.
  6. Freeze the mixture: Transfer it to a freezer-safe container, cover it, and freeze for at least 6 hours or until firm.
  7. Serve and enjoy: Let the ice cream soften slightly before scooping it into bowls.

Helpful Tips

  • Use cold whipping cream for a better texture.
  • Fold gently to keep the mixture light and airy.
  • Store the ice cream covered in the freezer.
  • Try adding cocoa powder for a chocolate flavor.
