FRESH EGG SALAD

A creamy, refreshing egg salad that's simple to prepare!

About This Recipe

Egg salad is a simple and delicious dish made with boiled eggs, mayonnaise, and fresh seasonings. It is perfect for lunch, sandwiches, or a light meal. This easy recipe brings together creamy texture and fresh flavors.

Preparation

10 minutes

Cooking

10 minutes

Servings

2 people

Ingredients

Prepare these ingredients before you start cooking:

  • 4 large eggs
  • 3 tablespoons mayonnaise
  • 1 teaspoon mustard (optional)
  • 2 tablespoons finely chopped onion
  • 1 tablespoon chopped fresh parsley
  • Salt to taste
  • Black pepper to taste
  • Lettuce leaves for serving (optional)

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Boil the eggs: Place the eggs in a saucepan, cover them with water, and bring the water to a boil. Cook until the eggs are fully hard-boiled.
  2. Cool the eggs: Transfer the cooked eggs to cold water and allow them to cool before peeling.
  3. Peel and chop: Remove the shells and chop the eggs into small pieces.
  4. Prepare the dressing: In a bowl, mix the mayonnaise, mustard, chopped onion, salt, and black pepper.
  5. Combine the ingredients: Add the chopped eggs and parsley to the dressing. Mix gently until everything is combined.
  6. Chill the salad: Refrigerate the salad briefly to let the flavors combine. Keep it refrigerated until serving.
  7. Serve and enjoy: Serve your egg salad on fresh lettuce or use it as a filling for sandwiches.

Helpful Tips

  • Allow the eggs to cool before mixing them with mayonnaise.
  • Add a little lemon juice for a fresh flavor.
  • Keep the egg salad refrigerated and do not leave it at room temperature for more than two hours.
