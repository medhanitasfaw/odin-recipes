HOMEMADE LASAGNA

A comforting Italian-inspired dish with layers of pasta and cheese!

About This Recipe

Lasagna is a popular baked pasta dish made with layers of pasta sheets, rich tomato sauce, seasoned ground beef, and creamy cheese. It is a satisfying meal for family dinners and special occasions. Follow these steps to prepare a delicious homemade lasagna.

Preparation

25 minutes

Cooking

45 minutes

Servings

4 people

Ingredients

Prepare the following ingredients:

  • 8 lasagna pasta sheets, or enough for your baking dish
  • 300 g ground beef
  • 1 small onion, finely chopped
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 cups tomato sauce
  • 1 cup shredded mozzarella cheese
  • 1/2 cup grated Parmesan cheese
  • 1 tablespoon cooking oil
  • 1 teaspoon dried oregano
  • Salt and black pepper to taste

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven: Preheat your oven to 180°C (350°F).
  2. Cook the pasta: Prepare the lasagna sheets according to the package instructions if they require pre-cooking.
  3. Cook the onion and garlic: Heat the oil in a pan. Add the chopped onion and garlic, then cook until softened.
  4. Prepare the meat sauce: Add the ground beef and cook until browned and thoroughly cooked. Stir in the tomato sauce, oregano, salt, and black pepper. Simmer for 10 minutes.
  5. Prepare the first layer: Spread a thin layer of meat sauce over the bottom of a baking dish.
  6. Build the layers: Add pasta sheets, meat sauce, and mozzarella. Repeat the layers until the ingredients are used, finishing with sauce and cheese on top.
  7. Bake the lasagna: Cover the dish with foil and bake for about 25 minutes. Remove the foil and bake for another 15–20 minutes, until bubbling and golden.
  8. Let it rest: Allow the lasagna to rest for about 10 minutes before cutting it into portions.
  9. Serve and enjoy: Serve warm and enjoy your homemade lasagna.

Helpful Tips

  • Do not add too much sauce, or the lasagna may become watery.
  • Let the lasagna rest before slicing to help it hold its shape.
  • Make sure the ground beef is thoroughly cooked.
  • Use a baking dish suitable for your oven.
