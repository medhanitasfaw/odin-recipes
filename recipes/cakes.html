HOMEMADE VANILLA CAKE

About Recipe

Enjoy a soft, fluffy homemade vanilla cake with a sweet aroma and delicious flavor. This simple cake is perfect for birthdays, family gatherings, or an afternoon treat. Serve it plain or decorate it with your favorite frosting.

Preparation time:

20 minutes

Cooking time:

30-35 minutes

Servings:

8 slices

Difficulty:

Easy

Ingredients

Prepare these ingredients before you start cooking:

  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 cup granulated sugar
  • ½ cup unsalted butter, softened
  • 2 large eggs
  • ¾ cup milk
  • 2 teaspoons baking powder
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • ¼ teaspoon salt

Optional decoration:

  • Powdered sugar for dusting
  • Fresh strawberries or other fruit

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Prepare the oven: Preheat your oven to 180°C (350°F). Grease a cake pan and lightly dust it with flour.
  2. Mix the dry ingredients: In a bowl, combine the flour, baking powder, and salt. Mix them well.
  3. Cream the butter and sugar: In another bowl, beat the softened butter and sugar until the mixture becomes light and fluffy
  4. Add the cheese: Place a slice of cheese on each hot patty and allow it to melt.
  5. Add the eggs and vanilla: Add the eggs one at a time, mixing well after each addition. Stir in the vanilla extract.
  6. Combine the ingredients: Gradually add the dry ingredients to the butter mixture, alternating with the milk. Mix until just combined; avoid overmixing
  7. Pour into the pan: Pour the batter into the prepared cake pan and spread it evenly.
  8. Bake the cake: Bake for approximately 30-35 minutes, until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean.
  9. Cool the cake: Remove the cake from the oven and allow it to cool in the pan for about 10 minutes. Then transfer it to a cooling rack.
  10. Decorate and serve: Once completely cool, dust the cake with powdered sugar or decorate it with fresh fruit. Cut into slices and enjoy!

Helpful Tips

  • Measure your ingredients carefully for the best results.
  • Use room-temperature eggs and milk for a smoother batter.
  • Avoid opening the oven frequently while the cake is baking.
  • Let the cake cool completely before decorating it.
