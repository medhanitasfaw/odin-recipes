HOMEMADE BURGER

A delicious homemade burger for a satisfying meal!

About This Recipe

Enjoy a juicy, homemade burger made with a seasoned beef patty, fresh vegetables, melted cheese, and a soft bun. This simple recipe is perfect for lunch or dinner and can be customized with your favorite toppings.

preparation

15 minutes

cooking

15 minutes

serving

2 burgers

Ingredients

Prepare these ingredients before you start cooking:

  • 2 burger buns
  • 250 g ground beef
  • 2 slices of cheese
  • 2 lettuce leaves
  • 1 tomato, sliced
  • 1 small onion, sliced
  • 2 tablespoons mayonnaise
  • Salt and black pepper to taste
  • 1 teaspoon cooking oil

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Prepare the beef: Season the ground beef with salt and black pepper. Shape it into two burger patties.
  2. Heat the pan: Add the cooking oil to a pan and heat it over medium-high heat.
  3. Cook the patties: Cook the patties until they are thoroughly cooked throughout. For ground beef, the safe internal temperature is 71°C (160°F).
  4. Add the cheese: Place a slice of cheese on each hot patty and allow it to melt.
  5. Prepare the buns: Cut the burger buns in half and lightly toast the cut sides if desired.
  6. Assemble the burger: Spread mayonnaise on the buns. Add the lettuce, cooked patty, tomato, and onion.
  7. Serve and enjoy: Place the top bun over the fillings and serve your homemade burger while warm.

Helpful Tips

  • Use fresh vegetables for the best flavor.
  • Customize your burger with your favorite sauces.
  • Wash your hands and keep raw meat separate from ready-to-eat ingredients.
